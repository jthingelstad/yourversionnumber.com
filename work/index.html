Bookmark the page and it recalculates every business morning. Add colleagues to it — your team, your department, everyone who started the same week — and the whole roster accrues in one place. No approval is required.

Nine presentation formats are cleared for internal use: an out-of-office reply, a timesheet on green-bar fanfold, a stock ticker of your career, a slide that says CONFIDENTIAL and offers three bullets. The birthday themes are not available here, and frankly would not be appropriate.

  • Timesheet

    Green-bar fanfold, an off-register stamp, and totals that are always one day out.

  • OOO

    An out-of-office auto-reply, signed by hand. Back never.

  • Ticker

    A live ticker on a black trading screen. Your career, but as a stock.
