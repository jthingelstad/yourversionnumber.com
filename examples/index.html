Examples

Eight groups, one to eight people, every one of them a real page you can open.

The point of putting everyone on a single page is the comparison: whose major version is about to roll over, who shares a birthday with whom, how far apart two people really are once you stop counting in years. Each example below is a live link — open one, edit the names, and it becomes yours.

  • Tim Berners-Lee

    1 person · zen theme

    One person, one number. He gave the web its addresses; this is his in three parts.

    Tim Berners-Lee

  • The Williams sisters

    2 people · polaroid theme

    Fifteen months apart, and you can read the gap straight off the minor version.

    Venus Williams, Serena Williams

  • The Harry Potter three

    3 people · tarot theme

    Cast as eleven-year-olds in 2001. All three are well into their fourth major release now.

    Rupert Grint, Daniel Radcliffe, Emma Watson

  • The Obamas

    4 people · family theme

    What the site was built for: a household on one page, parents and children on the same scale.

    Barack, Michelle, Malia, Sasha

  • Radiohead

    5 people · galaxy theme

    Same school, same band since 1985, and still inside a four-year spread.

    Philip Selway, Ed O’Brien, Thom Yorke, Colin Greenwood, Jonny Greenwood

  • The original six

    6 people · comic theme

    Two of them share a birthday seventeen years apart, so their patch numbers move in lockstep forever.

    Robert Downey Jr., Mark Ruffalo, Jeremy Renner, Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson

  • BTS

    7 people · memphis theme

    Seven people born inside five years. The minor versions bunch up; the patches scatter.

    Jin, Suga, J-Hope, RM, Jimin, V, Jungkook

  • Eight language creators

    8 people · terminal theme

    The people who gave us MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH in the first place, rendered in it.

    Bjarne Stroustrup, Larry Wall, James Gosling, Guido van Rossum, Anders Hejlsberg, Brendan Eich, Yukihiro Matsumoto, Rasmus Lerdorf

Every birthday here is public record and was checked against published sources. Want your own? Start an empty one and add people as you go, or browse the theme gallery first.
