Send someone their number

Put a note on it and send it a few days early: the card counts down, rolls over on the morning itself, and keeps climbing afterwards. Make one — no account, and the link lasts 400 days.

The URL is the whole thing

Entering a date stores nothing at all. Everything you type lives in the URL and nowhere else, which means a bookmark is your saved view. Cards are the one exception, and About says exactly what they keep.

Twenty-eight themes

Every view can be dressed up: a thermal receipt, a split-flap board, a nixie tube bank, a fridge door, a stock ticker, an out-of-office reply. All twenty-eight work in both editions — browse the gallery or see them carrying real people.
