How old are you, in semver?

Software gets a version number. People should too.

0.0.…

This site has one too. yourversionnumber.com shipped on 2026-05-01, which puts it comfortably pre-1.0.

Software is versioned MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. A major bump signals an incompatible change. Minor bumps add features but stay backwards-compatible. Patches are small fixes. People work the same way, so a person’s version number is their age written the same way: MAJOR is your decade, MINOR is your age inside that decade, and PATCH is the days since your most recent birthday.

Someone who is 46 years old and 52 days past their birthday is on 4.6.52. Tomorrow they ship 4.6.53. On their birthday, 4.7.0.

Two editions

  • 4.6.52 Birthday Edition

    The original. Your age, from your birthday. Add as many people as you like — the whole family fits on one page.

  • 3.2.40 Work Edition™

    The same idea, applied to a job: years of tenure, quarter within the tenure year, and business days into that quarter. Weekends don’t tick.

The URL is the whole thing

There is no account, no server, and no database. Everything you enter lives in the URL and nowhere else, which means a bookmark is your saved view, and sharing the link shares exactly what you see. It also means anyone with the link can read the dates in it, so don’t put anything sensitive in there. More on how that works in About.

Every number, in costume
