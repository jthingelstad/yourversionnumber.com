Write someone a card

Their birthday, a theme, and a note in your own words. The link is the card — nothing is saved here.

Reads as —

Everything you write travels in the link, readable by whoever you send it to — and by anyone they forward it to. In the last seven days before the day itself, the card counts down.

A joke by Jamie Thingelstad from 2018 that turned out to be useful. Source on GitHub.

Counting your age this way was David Hussman’s idea. He died in August 2018, six months after the post. This is kept in his memory.
